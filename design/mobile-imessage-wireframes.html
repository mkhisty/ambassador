iMessage conversation wireframes

A rough screen map for a messaging-first assistant for startup and general outreach. It keeps the user in control of identity, contact context, every outgoing message, and every commitment.

Build notes

Suggested MVP build order

  1. Website sign-in, secure conversation linking, and selected Notion, Sheets, and Gmail connections.
  2. Campaign brief and contact list with source-linked notes.
  3. Draft, edit and discard loop; require explicit approval before each send.
  4. Inbound reply recap and human-approved follow-up draft.
  5. Schedule proposal, timezone display, invite approval, delivery status.
  6. Document attachment and extraction only after privacy, parsing and review behavior are validated.

State coverage to design and test

  • Identity unverified, verification pending, verified, account mismatch.
  • Contact source present, stale, missing or conflicting; extraction requires confirmation.
  • Draft unsaved, edited, approved, sent, discarded.
  • Reply received, unclear, or asking about sensitive terms.
  • Schedule proposed, edited, approved, sent, delivery uncertain, failed, recipient accepted or declined.
  • All outbound commitments remain pending until the campaign owner explicitly approves them.

Conceptual cards describe a possible product direction. They do not claim that document extraction, external email, calendar access, message delivery, or any named integration currently exists. Hermes transport role requires confirmation. Notion, Sheets, Gmail and calendar integrations shown here are conceptual and make no external calls. The iMessage chrome is illustrative.
